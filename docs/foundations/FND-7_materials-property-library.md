## 0. Purpose

The **constitutive spine** is the single, continuous provider of every material property the field
solver needs — **equation of state** (p, e), **electrical & thermal conductivity**, **viscosity**,
**opacity** (Planck/Rosseland), and **charged-particle stopping** — as one law over the medium-state
vector `M`, for **arbitrary materials, from cold solid to hot dense plasma, with no discontinuity at
any phase or regime boundary** (Rule 12). It replaces the old per-`material_id` handbook library, whose
per-material tables with hard validity ranges were a Rule-12 seam (Ben, 2026-07-19: *"don't hard-code
magic numbers for solid metal then give up at molten metal — smooth computation, essentially all
materials computable"*).

FND-7 is the **spec** — what the spine provides and the invariants it must satisfy. The **offline
generator** that computes it (the staged Saha/QEOS → DFT-average-atom backbone, §5) is **OFFL-5**; the on-disk table
format/interpolation is **FND-5**. Read after FND-1 (`M`, `UncertainInput`, `ValidityEnvelope`) and
FND-5 (the table mechanism). Consumed by every operator (SOLV-1/2/3) via `M`.

## 1. Scope & razor ruling

**Owns:** the set of constitutive quantities and their representation; the **one-backbone, no-cliff
mandate**; the **hybrid data-calibration** contract; the per-regime accuracy/uncertainty declaration;
the composition/mixture rule.

**Defers (owner named):**
- The **offline computation** (DFT average-atom solve, closures, table generation) → **OFFL-5**.
- The **table on-disk format, loader, interpolation & interpolation-error budget** → **FND-5** (the
  spine is a client of that schema; tables are keyed on *state*, not `material_id`).
- The **uncertainty type** it carries → **FND-1**; **provenance/pedigree** scoring → **COUP-6**.
- How a cell holds composition and reconstructs `M` → **FND-2** (`U` → `M` each step).
- The **degradation physics** that consumes limits (DPA/burnup/corrosion) → **SOLV-8** (FND-7 supplies
  the limits + the properties; SOLV-8 marches the clocks).

**Razor ruling:** infrastructure, but load-bearing for S8 — it is the constitutive foundation whose
validation-by-parts underwrites every untested-regime prediction (META-1 §4.1). Its fidelity
obligation is that the *law be continuous over all of `M`* so no operator is ever forced to branch on a
material or regime (which would be a seam), and that its error be **declared, not cliffed**.

## 3. Method

### 3.1 One backbone for all four quantities — DFT average-atom *(the Rule-12 core)*

A **Kohn-Sham DFT average-atom (AA) model** (Purgatorio/Tartarus/INFERNO lineage; prototyped from the
open-source Python **atoMEC**) is the single electronic-structure solve from which EOS, transport, and
opacity are *all* derived consistently — thermodynamic derivatives of the AA free energy give EOS; a
Kubo-Greenwood integral over the same continuum states gives conductivity; the same self-consistent
orbitals give opacity; and the AA electron density + dielectric response feed stopping. **One backbone,
four quantities, no independent per-quantity model** — the data-model expression of "one law over `M`."
*(Staging: the backbone **slot** is filled by the Saha/QEOS analytic model at W2/W3 and upgraded to DFT-AA
in a later wave — §5, D-E; every stage satisfies the same invariants.)* [META-3: `dft-avg-atom`, `atomec`,
`purgatorio-kg`]

### 3.2 Equation of state — wide-range, cold-curve-anchored, with a vapor dome

- **Primary:** AA free-energy derivatives (few-% in hot dense matter). **Fast fallback / cross-check
  for materials not yet AA-solved:** **QEOS** (Thomas-Fermi electrons + Cowan ion model). [META-3:
  `qeos`, `dft-avg-atom`]
- **Cold-curve anchor (non-negotiable):** a QEOS-style **bonding/cold-curve correction** ties the
  statistical model to the correct room-temperature solid (ρ₀, bulk modulus) — additive and smooth, so
  it never introduces a cliff. Without it the "simple" cold solid is quietly wrong. [META-3: `qeos`]
- **Vapor dome:** a **FEOS-style Maxwell-construction** liquid-vapor coexistence, because a propulsion
  exhaust traverses expansion/vaporization and a bare TF model fails exactly there. [META-3: `feos`]
- **Thermodynamic consistency (S11, the physical requirement):** the spine EOS is stored and carried as a
  **thermodynamic potential — Helmholtz F(ρ,T) per species** — with p, e, s, and sound speed obtained by
  **consistent differentiation of the one interpolant**, never independently interpolated: Maxwell relations
  hold by construction, and convexity (thermodynamic stability) is auditable — no imaginary sound speeds
  near the vapor dome. The offline **convexity / sound-speed-positivity audit** is a generation obligation
  (OFFL-5), recorded in table metadata; the schema + interpolation rule are FND-5 §3.3's. [META-3:
  `helmholtz-table`]

### 3.3 Transport — one continuous formula each, cold metal → plasma

- **Electrical & thermal conductivity:** **Lee-More-Desjarlais** — one continuous formula spanning cold
  metal → plasma, its **Saha↔Thomas-Fermi ionization blend** the worked no-cliff template through the
  metal-insulator transition (recovers Spitzer hot, degenerate-metal cold). Kubo-Greenwood-on-AA where
  the full solve is paid for. [META-3: `lee-more-desjarlais`, `purgatorio-kg`]
- **Viscosity & ion transport:** **Stanton-Murillo** algebraic fits (self-diffusion, interdiffusion,
  viscosity, ion thermal conductivity) from weak→strong coupling, fed by AA-derived ⟨Z⟩ + screening
  length — so they share the backbone. [META-3: `stanton-murillo`]

### 3.4 Opacity — average-atom means, calibrated to detailed tables

Planck/Rosseland means from the **same AA orbitals** (free-free + bound-free + bound-bound), so opacity
is another response of the spine, not a separate model. Calibrated toward **TOPS/OPLIB** detailed-line
tables where they exist (§3.6); **declare wider bands on Planck/line-dominated cases**, where AA means
are least accurate. [META-3: `aa-opacity`, `tops-oplib`]

### 3.5 Stopping — the same backbone

Charged-particle stopping = **RPA-LDA average-atom + Li-Petrasso + BPS** (already selected, META-3
§6.1), which consume the **same AA electron density + dielectric response** — one continuous S(E,
species; `M`) with no cold↔plasma join. This is why stopping lives in the spine, not a separate module.
[META-3: `stopping-rpa-lda`, `stopping-li-petrasso`, `stopping-bps`]

**The spine's `stopping` output is a collision-coefficient bundle, not a scalar** *(v0.3, for SOLV-3)*.
A fast-particle transport operator (SOLV-3) needs, besides the drag `S = dE/dx`, the **velocity-space
diffusion** (pitch-angle scattering ν_⊥, energy straggling D_∥) and the effective charge `Z_eff(v;M)`.
By fluctuation-dissipation these are the *same* AA dielectric response that yields `S` — so the spine
returns `{electronic drag, nuclear/ion drag, D_∥, D_⊥/ν_pitch, Z_eff(v;M)}` from the one solve. Were the
diffusion computed in SOLV-3 instead, it would be a private scattering law — a Rule-12 seam. SOLV-3 owns
the *transport*; every *coefficient* is a spine query.

### 3.6 Hybrid data-calibration — the no-cliff guarantee

Where high-quality data exist (CINDAS/TPRC solid conductivity, NIST-JANAF, shock-Hugoniot EOS, MMPDS
allowables), the physics model is **smoothly nudged toward the data** by a **coverage-weighted Gaussian-
process (Kennedy-O'Hagan) discrepancy** in the same `M`-coordinates:
- **Multiplicative** correction for positive-definite quantities (conductivity, opacity, pressure),
  **additive** for signed/energy quantities — preserving positivity and asymptotic scaling.
- A stationary-kernel GP **reverts to its prior mean (zero correction) far from data**, so the blend
  back to the pure physics model is automatic and smooth — **the mathematical guarantee against a
  "calibration cliff."** The GP **posterior variance is the data-coverage-weighted error band** (widens
  where data are sparse). The GP is **physics-constrained** (log-space for positive quantities,
  monotonicity/thermo limits) so it never produces p<0 or κ<0 in extrapolation. [META-3: `ko-discrepancy`]

Result: a material is `(fundamental data + optional smooth GP correction)`; the property law is one
continuous function of `M` with handbook accuracy where data exist and physically-smooth extrapolation
everywhere else. **All materials are computable; none has a validity cliff — only wider, declared bands.**

### 3.7 Composition & mixtures — tabulation coordinates and the exact mixture rule *(S12)*

**Tabulation coordinates, per quantity:** per-species EOS as **Helmholtz F_s(ρ_s, T)** (§3.2);
electrical/thermal conductivity, viscosity, and opacity vs **(ρ, T, ⟨Z⟩)**; stopping/collision-coefficient
bundles vs (E, projectile species; ρ, T, ⟨Z⟩). The full `M` is never a table axis — `M` is reduced to these
coordinates at evaluation. (Generated in exactly these coordinates by OFFL-5 §3.2.)

**Mixture rule (exact, deterministic):** multi-species cells combine per-species sub-EOS by **isobaric
additive-volume pressure equilibration**: given cell (ρ, T, {w_s}), solve for partial densities ρ_s with
p_s(ρ_s, T) equal across species and Σ w_s/ρ_s = 1/ρ, by a bounded Newton iteration with a **fixed
iteration count** (a named constant — determinism, META-1 §2; never a convergence-dependent step count).
**Mix/derive order (stated):** (1) equilibrate sub-volumes (fixed-count iteration); (2) derive mixture EOS
quantities from the equilibrated sub-states (common p; mass-weighted e and s; sound speed from the
additive-volume mixture compressibility); (3) evaluate transport/opacity at the equilibrated sub-states and
combine (Stanton-Murillo ion-mixture rule for ion transport; the per-quantity mixture rule otherwise) —
no per-mixture special case (FND-2 §3.3). Phase transitions appear as smooth features of the EOS surface
(latent heat via the free energy), **not** hand-coded breakpoints.

## 4. Coupling relationships
- **FND-2** reconstructs `M` from `U` each step and calls the spine; the spine never sees a material
  label, only `M`.
- **OFFL-5** generates the spine tables offline (staged Saha/QEOS → DFT-AA backbone + closures + GP
  calibration, §5) into the FND-5 HDF5 schema, keyed on the §3.7 state coordinates.
- **FND-5** stores/interpolates the tables and enforces the (wide) validity envelope; the interpolation
  bound is **declared table metadata consumed by COUP-5 as an epistemic interval** into the p-box (FND-5
  §3.4) — never RSS'd with the spine's physical band, never attached to an inner-loop scalar (S14).
- **SOLV-1/2/3** consume EOS/transport, opacity, and stopping respectively; **SOLV-8** consumes
  degradation limits.
- **COUP-6** scores pedigree partly from spine provenance (data-anchored regions score higher than
  pure-model extrapolation); **VAL-1/2** validate the spine at the **unit-physics tier** (§6).

## 5. Uncertainty & validity

**Milestone staging (D-E, Ben 2026-08-14).** The spine backbone from **day one (W2/W3)** is the
**Saha/QEOS analytic model over the full (ρ,T) range** — smooth everywhere, honest **~10–20% bands** — with
tabulated experimental data **GP-blended on top where data exist** (§3.6: coverage-weighted, reverting to
the analytic model outside data — the no-cliff guarantee). The spine's envelope is therefore **never the
data envelope**: an ionizing-steam (NSWR-class) query off the data corner gets the analytic backbone with
its declared band, not a refusal cliff. The **full DFT-AA backbone (§3.1) is the later-wave upgrade of the
same slot**, tightening the WDM valley; every stage satisfies the same invariants (continuity, no-cliff,
declared bands). Generation plan → OFFL-5 §3.1/§3.2. [META-3: `saha-qeos`]

The spine is a **first-class uncertainty source** feeding UQ (FND-1). Its band is regime-dependent and
declared (feeds the S8 p-box):
- **Cold solid:** EOS good *only if cold-curve-anchored*; conductivity good for simple metals, weakest
  at the metal-insulator transition; opacity least reliable (band/molecular effects AA misses) →
  **widest bands here despite "simple" physics**.
- **Warm dense matter (the hard valley):** the dominant-uncertainty regime — EOS few-to-15%, transport
  20-50% model spread, opacity ~2× on Planck. Where calibration data buy the most.
- **Hot plasma:** converges to controlled limits (Spitzer, ideal-Saha, Kramers) → tightest bands.

Validity is **as wide as the model supports** (the spine's raison d'être); out-of-envelope still
refuses/flags (FND-5 §3.5) — but "envelope" now means "beyond where even the AA model is trusted," a far
wider window than a handbook T-range. This module often dominates a spread for advanced concepts — Sobol
(COUP-5) will say so.

## 6. Validation plan *(unit-physics tier, VAL-1)*
1. **EOS:** reproduce shock-Hugoniot data (principal Hugoniot) for reference materials; cold-curve
   matches ρ₀/bulk modulus; no kink in d(p)/dρ across pressure ionization.
2. **Transport:** conductivity reproduces measured solid-metal values and DFT-MD/experiment in WDM;
   Spitzer limit recovered hot; continuous across the metal-insulator transition.
3. **Opacity:** Rosseland/Planck means agree with TOPS/OPLIB within declared bands.
4. **Stopping:** reproduces ASTAR/PSTAR (cold) and plasma stopping data (hot), continuous across.
5. **No-cliff test:** every property and its first derivative are continuous across melt, vaporization,
   ionization, and the metal-insulator transition (the Rule-12 seam test applied to the spine).
6. **Calibration:** the GP correction reverts to the pure model outside data coverage (no cliff), and
   its posterior variance tracks data density.
